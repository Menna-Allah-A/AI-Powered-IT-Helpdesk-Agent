
DOCUMENT TITLE: Outlook Missing Messages Troubleshooting
CATEGORY: Email & Collaboration
PLATFORM: Outlook.com
SCOPE: Missing emails, deleted messages, inbox rules, filters, and account activity

==================================================
1. OVERVIEW
==================================================

This document covers troubleshooting steps when messages appear to be missing from an Outlook.com inbox.

==================================================
2. CHECK OTHER EMAIL FOLDERS
==================================================

Check the following locations:

- Junk Email: The message might have been classified as junk.
- Other: If Focused Inbox is enabled, the message might be in the Other tab.
- Deleted Items: The message might have been deleted.
- Archive: The message might have been archived instead of deleted.

==================================================
3. RECOVER DELETED MESSAGES
==================================================

STEP 1: Open the Deleted Items folder.

STEP 2: Look for the missing message.

STEP 3: If it is not there, select Recover items deleted from this folder.

IMPORTANT:

- Messages in Deleted Items may be recoverable for up to 30 days, according to the source.
- The recovery option must be accessed from a computer.
- The source notes a limitation for child accounts.

==================================================
4. CHECK EMAIL FILTERS
==================================================

Check whether inbox filters are hiding messages.

Review the configured filters and determine whether they are preventing the expected messages from appearing in the inbox.

==================================================
5. CHECK INBOX RULES AND SWEEP
==================================================

Inbox rules and Sweep can automatically move or delete messages.

- Review existing inbox rules.
- Check whether a rule is moving messages to another folder.
- Check whether Sweep is affecting messages.
- Use a computer to edit inbox rules when required.

==================================================
6. CHECK ACCOUNT SIGN-IN ACTIVITY
==================================================

Review recent account sign-in activity to identify unusual access.

The source recommends checking activity from the last 30 days.

If suspicious activity is found, investigate the account's security.

==================================================
7. CHECK ACCOUNT INACTIVITY
==================================================

The source describes the following Outlook.com inactivity periods:

- An Outlook.com mailbox may close after one year without signing in.
- A Microsoft account may close after two years of inactivity.

==================================================
8. TROUBLESHOOTING SUMMARY
==================================================

| Symptom | What to check |
| Email missing from inbox | Junk Email, Other, Archive |
| Message was deleted | Deleted Items and recovery |
| Message moved unexpectedly | Inbox rules and Sweep |
| Messages are hidden | Inbox filters |
| Unexpected account activity | Recent sign-in activity |
| Account inaccessible after inactivity | Outlook.com and Microsoft account inactivity periods |

END OF DOCUMENT
