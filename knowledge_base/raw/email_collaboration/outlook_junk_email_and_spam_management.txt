
DOCUMENT TITLE: Outlook Junk Email and Spam Management
SOURCE: Microsoft Support
CATEGORY: Email & Collaboration
APPLICABILITY: Outlook on the Web and New Outlook for Windows

==================================================
1. OVERVIEW
==================================================

Outlook can automatically move messages identified as possible junk email into the Junk Email folder.

Any malware contained in these messages is disabled.

Users can manage unwanted email by blocking senders and domains or adding trusted senders to the Safe Senders list.

==================================================
2. BLOCK UNWANTED SENDERS
==================================================

Users can block specific email addresses or entire domains.

Messages from blocked senders are moved directly to the Junk Email folder.

To block a sender:

1. Open Outlook Settings.
2. Select Mail > Junk email.
3. Under Blocked senders and domains, select Add blocked sender.
4. Enter the email address or domain.
5. Select OK.
6. Select Save.

To block all messages from a domain, enter the domain name.

IMPORTANT:
Junk Email messages are retained for 30 days before being automatically deleted. According to this source, these messages are not recoverable after automatic deletion.

==================================================
3. UNBLOCK SENDERS
==================================================

To remove an email address or domain from the blocked senders list:

1. Open Outlook Settings.
2. Select Mail > Junk email.
3. Select the address or domain to unblock.
4. Select the Remove option.

==================================================
4. ADD SAFE SENDERS
==================================================

Messages from addresses or domains on the Safe Senders list are not moved to the Junk Email folder.

To add a trusted sender:

1. Open Outlook Settings.
2. Select Mail > Junk email.
3. Under Safe senders and domains, select Add safe sender.
4. Enter the trusted email address or domain.
5. Select OK.
6. Select Save.

==================================================
5. EDIT OR REMOVE SENDER LIST ENTRIES
==================================================

Users can edit or remove addresses and domains from both lists.

1. Open the relevant list under Mail > Junk email.
2. Select the email address or domain.
3. Choose Edit or Remove.

==================================================
6. EMPTY THE JUNK EMAIL FOLDER
==================================================

To manually delete all messages in the Junk Email folder:

1. Open the folder list.
2. Select Junk Email.
3. Select Delete all at the top of the screen.

==================================================
7. IT SUPPORT NOTES
==================================================

When investigating unwanted email or messages incorrectly classified as junk, identify:

- Whether the sender is on the Blocked Senders list.
- Whether the sender should be added to Safe Senders.
- Whether the message is located in the Junk Email folder.
- Whether the user needs to unblock an address or domain.
- Whether messages were automatically deleted after the retention period.

IMPORTANT:
This document covers junk email management. It does not provide detailed procedures for recovering missing messages from Deleted Items, checking inbox rules, or troubleshooting synchronization.

END OF DOCUMENT
