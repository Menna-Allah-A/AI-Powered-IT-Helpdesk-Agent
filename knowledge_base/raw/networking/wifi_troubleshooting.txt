Wi-Fi Troubleshooting Guide

Problem: A company laptop cannot connect to Wi-Fi.

Troubleshooting steps:

1. Verify that Wi-Fi is enabled on the laptop.
2. Check whether airplane mode is enabled and disable it if necessary.
3. Forget the Wi-Fi network and reconnect using the correct credentials.
4. Restart the wireless adapter.
5. Check whether other devices are experiencing the same issue.
6. If multiple devices are affected, the issue may be related to the router or network infrastructure.
7. If the issue persists, contact IT support and create a support ticket.

Do not ask employees to share their passwords with IT support.
